OUR ORIGINS

A Heritage Founded on Craftsmanship

Established in 2012 in Bandra West, Mumbai, AURA & GOLD was conceived to solve a critical gap in the Indian salon industry: bringing international styling standards and clinical hygiene to urban professionals without sacrificing warm Indian hospitality.

Over the past 14 years, our brand has expanded into four flagship sanctuaries, employing over 120 certified hair directors, dermal therapists, and bridal artists.

GUIDING PRINCIPLES

The Four Pillars of AURA & GOLD

Every service, product choice, and client interaction is guided by our core values.

Authentic Artistry

We never compromise on technique. Every stylist completes 200+ hours of annual advanced training.

Clinical Hygiene

Hospital-grade UV autoclaves, single-use capes, and daily room sanitization protocols.

Organic Formulations

100% genuine ammonia-free hair colors, dermatologically tested facials, and Kerala oils.

Personalized Care

No rushed appointments. Every service starts with a private 15-minute consultation.

SAFETY FIRST

Hospital-Grade Sanitization Standards

Your health and peace of mind are non-negotiable. Every outlet operates under strict clinical sanitization guidelines overseen by our dedicated hygiene director.

UV Autoclave Tool Sterilization: Metal scissors, combs, and tweezers sterilized after every single client.
Single-Use Client Kits: Disposable biodegradable capes, towels, and manicure files.
HEPA Air Filtration: Continuous indoor air purification across all facial and spa rooms.
