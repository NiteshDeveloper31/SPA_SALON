ESTABLISHED 2012

Crafting Royal Indian Pampering Experiences

Founded in Mumbai's prestigious Bandra neighborhood in 2012, AURA & GOLD was conceived with a single ambitious vision: to create an Indian salon experience that stands shoulder-to-shoulder with global luxury spa hotels while celebrating Indian beauty tradition.

We recognized that Indian hair textures, skin melanin profiles, and grand wedding traditions required specialized care. Rather than applying generic Western salon templates, we created proprietary treatments combining London & Paris hair coloring techniques with authentic Kashmiri Saffron, Kumkumadi oils, and pure Kerala Ayurveda.

Today, across 12 flagship sanctuaries in Mumbai, Bengaluru, New Delhi, and Hyderabad, our 28+ master artists have served over 15,000 brides and 100,000+ salon guests.
