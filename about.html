OUR STORY

Beauty, Wellness & The Art of Feeling Your Best

Conceived as a tranquil urban refuge, AURA & GOLD blends international hair science with authentic Indian body wellness traditions to deliver bespoke, unhurried care.

THE BEGINNING

More Than A Salon. A Place To Reconnect With Yourself.

AURA & GOLD was established in 2012 out of a simple observation: urban life in India's major metros moves fast, yet beauty and self-care appointments were often rushed, impersonal, and transactional.

Our founders set out to build a sanctuary where hair styling, clinical skincare, and Ayurvedic massage therapies coexist in harmony. We believed that true radiance cannot be achieved by superficial treatments alone—it requires an atmosphere of deep mental calm and genuine personal attention.

Today, across our flagship sanctuaries in Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, and Hyderabad, every service begins with a private consultation. We take the time to understand your unique hair texture, skin concerns, and stress levels before beginning any treatment.
